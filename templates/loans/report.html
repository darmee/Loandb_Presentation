How to use this report
  1. Headline: how the period went against the one before. Green arrows are good news, red are bad, whichever way the number moved.
  2. Decision checklist: each line is a question the business should be able to answer. Red needs a decision or an owner, amber needs watching, green is fine.
  3. The detail (months, products, pipeline, rejections, Cash for Car) explains the flags. To see the actual applications behind any number, open the dashboard with the same dates and click it.
  4. Notes for the meeting (last page): write down what was decided, who owns it and by when, then check it in the next report.
Every application is counted in the period it was submitted in and followed to today, exactly as on the dashboard's Overview and Compare pages.
